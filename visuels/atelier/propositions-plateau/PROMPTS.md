# Prompts — trois orientations du plateau Charmed

Explorations demandées par Aurore. Génération par outil intégré image_gen, sans modification du jeu. Les faces portent une illustration et un nom ; le contenu détaillé est réservé au verso. Aucun design n’est encore choisi.

## Proposition 1

Use case: ui-mockup. Create ONE spectacular but genuinely readable high-fidelity desktop strategy card game interface concept, landscape 1920x1200 or similar generous landscape. French interface for CHARMED original 1998 television series, aimed at an adult fan. A finished screen mockup, not a photograph of a monitor, no devices, no collage of multiple alternatives. Prioritize actual clear usable layout over decorative spectacle. Large readable cards with generous gaps, sharp editorial typography. Two opposed camps: COLE above, PHOEBE below; their two blue initial objective cards face one another across the central horizontal boundary. Top-to-bottom game area: enemy name and 3-card hand; enemy sub-state area; enemy initial objective; human initial objective; human sub-state area; 3-card human hand and human name. Sub-states below their root in the owner's reading direction, show 2 slots per camp (one occupied one dashed empty). One yellow key alongside a blue target and one red enemy lock directly beside its target IN THE TARGET'S territory, readable short connection. No crisscross spaghetti, no spreadsheet of tiny icons. Reserve a clear left area for distinct 'RADIO HALLIWELL' and 'L’ARBITRE', never merge their identities; concise radio 'JOUR 3 · Le programme est publié.' and arbiter 'Proposition acceptée.' Radio small vintage radio icon, arbiter scales icon. Small calendar strip J3 J5 J8. In enemy territory a clearly separate concise comment 'À toi de jouer, Phoebe.' Main status 'À VOUS DE JOUER' and '1 COUP DISPONIBLE'. Exact header 'CHARMED', subtitle 'La réception de minuit'. A subtle triquetra iconic to Charmed, NOT a pentagram.
CRITICAL CARDS: large pictorial faces with ONLY a rich illustration and ONE name label. Never front-face description text, rules, flavor text, stats, costs, numbers, prose, explanatory pretext. Character card 'Phoebe' with recognizable 1990s Alyssa Milano as Phoebe Halliwell, 'Piper' with Holly Marie Combs; enemy 'Cole' with Julian McMahon. Object cards 'Invitation' with illustrated envelope, 'Livre des Ombres' with green leather grimoire and triquetra, 'Potion' with glass bottle. NOT emoji, NOT generic fantasy warriors. Resource and state frames BLUE, key frames GOLD/YELLOW, locks RED; one unavailable hand card blue-grey. Blue initial state card names 'Élise captive' above and 'Élise en sécurité' below, images tell the story. Blue sub-state card named 'Confiance d’Élise'. Yellow key titled 'La rencontre' illustrated conversation. Red lock titled 'Accès fermé' illustrated barred door. The full content belongs ONLY on the BACK: optionally one separate clear card-back inspection panel headed 'VERSO · LA RENCONTRE', with 'Élise accepte de rencontrer Phoebe.'; all cards on board remain illustration + name only. Avoid overcrowding and all tiny illegible text. Strong hierarchy and clear ownership. This is a design proposal, not an official screenshot.
DIRECTION 1 — 'LE MANOIR'. Warm luxurious Halliwell manor aesthetic, Victorian walnut architectural trims, recognizable softly blurred attic windows and warm candles behind a flat usable surface; oxblood velvet accents, antique brass, midnight blue card fields and luminous cream text. Do not make parchment unreadably dark. Main game area divided into two broad rectangular horizontal territories, central opposing objectives in generous bands, branches as neatly spaced lateral rows, hand trays outside the branches. The left radio/arbitration sidebar is an elegant fixed carved-wood cabinet with two distinct clean panels. Decorative stained glass only on outer corners, no giant room taking space. Large cinematic hand-painted realistic portraits like refined Charmed collectible cards, not photos pasted on dashboards. Premium 1990s supernatural drama atmosphere, believable modern interaction clarity. Small concept label '01 — LE MANOIR' outside the game masthead.

DIRECTION 2 — 'LE LIVRE DES OMBRES'. Visually radically different: luminous antique ivory parchment, rich forest-green leather binding, fine moss-green ink ornament and restrained burnt gold. Charmed green Book of Shadows with triquetra, beautifully drawn botanical/magical marginalia ONLY at edges. The WHOLE play surface is a broad horizontal open grimoire seen perfectly straight-on as flat usable UI, not perspective. Wide cream pages make art and text extremely readable. Keep camps TOP and BOTTOM, NOT enemy left vs player right. Organize each camp as clean horizontal evidence lanes around a central objective spine; occupied blue sub-state left of spine and yellow key to its side, dashed empty slot right. A narrow left margin panel is an independent cream parchment ledger for radio and arbiter, clearly separated. Hand cards sit in forest-green padded horizontal trays at top and bottom. Sophisticated gouache/ink realistic character portrait illustrations, large, beautifully framed with blue/yellow/red enamel hairline borders. Show one flipped card back in an unobtrusive right margin inspection area with just the exact allowed back text. Subtle page fold, a few wax details, NO clutter from candles/books on top of playing area. Small concept label '02 — LE LIVRE DES OMBRES'.
