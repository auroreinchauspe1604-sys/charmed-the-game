Corners have restrained tiny gold engraved botanical scrolls confined to the outermost 4% of canvas. Warm cream parchment matching reference, softly irregular natural grain, subtly aged only at extreme edges. CENTRAL 88% OF IMAGE MUST BE EVEN-LIGHT, VERY LOW CONTRAST, ENTIRELY EMPTY, FREE OF ORNAMENTS, shadows, stains, drawings or creases, so variable-length dark text and interactive controls remain legible anywhere. ONE continuous writing field, NO open book center fold, NO two columns, NO separators, NO fixed text boxes, NO card slots, NO illustrations, NO triquetra, NO logo. Dark green leather frame around entire perimeter, cream parchment interior. No outer surrounding scene or board. No transparent holes or checkerboard. Keep the bottom as empty as the middle so controls can reflow and the panel can adapt to content. The deliverable itself is only the blank background with narrow border. Absolutely zero typography.
